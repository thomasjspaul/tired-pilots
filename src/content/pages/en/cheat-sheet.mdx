A condensed reference for **703, 704 and 705** operations. It may not cover every
situation, and your company may be stricter. Each item links to the full page,
which carries the citations and the verbatim regulation text. Not legal advice —
verify against the current CARs and your Operations Manual.

## Flight time — [700.27](/rules/700-27)

| Limit       | Window                                       |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| 112 hours   | any 28 consecutive days                      |
| 300 hours   | any 90 consecutive days                      |
| 1,000 hours | any 365 consecutive days                     |
| 8 hours     | any 24 consecutive hours — single-pilot only |

"Flight time" is block time (chocks-off to chocks-on) and includes **all** your
flying, for any operator. Running totals over the window ending now.

## Maximum flight duty period — [700.28](/rules/700-28)

Read from the tables by **start time** (at your acclimatized location), **number
of flights**, and **average flight duration**. Not acclimatized → use the local
time back in the zone you're still acclimatized to.

## Augmented crew — [700.60](/rules/700-60)

<AugmentedCrewTable />

Only for FDPs of **three flights or fewer**, with the required in-flight rest.
Rest afterwards is the longest of: the duty just completed, 16 h (ends at home
base), or 14 h (suitable accommodation).

## Hours of work — [700.29](/rules/700-29)

| Limit       | Window                                                                                                                          |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2,200 hours | any 365 days                                                                                                                    |
| 192 hours   | any 28 days                                                                                                                     |
| 60 hours    | any 7 days — with 1 single day free / 168 h + 4 / 672 h                                                                         |
| 70 hours    | any 7 days — with 120 h free (incl. 5 local nights' rest) / 504 h + no early/late/night duty, no FDP > 12 h, ≤ 24 h work / 48 h |

Reserve counts **33%**; standby counts **100%**.

## Rest — [700.40](/rules/700-40)

| FDP ends            | Minimum rest                                                     |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Home base           | 12 h · or 11 h + travel time · or 10 h in suitable accommodation |
| Away from home base | 10 h in suitable accommodation                                   |

- **Duty > 1 h past the max FDP:** rest = the longer of (max FDP + the overrun)
  or the normal rest.
- **Disruptive schedule** (flip between early and late/night duty):
  [+ one local night's rest](/rules/700-41) — unless > 4 h off your acclimatized
  zone, then [700.42](/rules/700-42).
- **Time zones** ([700.42](/rules/700-42)): 11 h / 14 h away from base by
  difference; 13 h or 1–3 local nights' rest returning to base.
- **Positioning after an FDP past the max** ([700.43](/rules/700-43)): rest =
  total hours of work (+ the excess over the max if that's > 3 h). Operator can't
  require > 3 h over without your agreement, and never > 7 h over.

**local night's rest** = ≥ 9 h between 22:30–09:30 acclimatized.
**single day free from duty** = from the start of one local night's rest to the
end of the next.

## Split flight duty — [700.50](/rules/700-50)

Break ≥ 60 min in suitable accommodation → FDP may exceed the 700.28 max by:

**(break − 45 min) × 100%** if the break is 00:00–05:59 acclimatized, or **× 50%**
otherwise. No cap on total FDP length. Night duty: max **3 consecutive nights**.
On reserve: RDP + 2 h, max 2 flights after the break.

## Reserve — [700.70](/rules/700-70)

- Notice of the RAP: **12 h** ahead (32 h if it touches your WOCL).
- RAP ≤ **14 h**; **10 h** rest between RAPs.
- Max **RDP** by start time: 18 h (02:00–17:59), 17 h (18:00–18:59),
  16 h (19:00–20:59), 15 h (21:00–22:59), 14 h (23:00–01:59).
- Augmented: up to 20 / 22 / 26 h (see the page).
- Quiet WOCL (RAP starts 02:00–05:59, no contact): + 2 h or 50% of the RAP in
  that window, whichever is less.

## Unforeseen operational circumstances — [700.63](/rules/700-63)

**PIC only**, after consulting the crew. Extend beyond the max by: **1 h**
single-pilot, **2 h** two-pilot, **3 h** augmented + 1 flight, **2 h** augmented +
2–3 flights. Rest afterwards grows by **at least** the extension. Event must be
within 60 min of, or during, the FDP and beyond the operator's control.

## Standby — [700.71](/rules/700-71)

Sheltered place to sit with food and drink. Released without a flight → same rest
as after an FDP (12 h / 11 h + travel / 10 h at home base; 10 h away).

## Controlled rest on the flight deck — [700.72](/rules/700-72)

≤ 45 min, in cruise, done ≥ 30 min before descent, only one crew member at a time,
≥ 2 on the flight deck. Hand over duties and brief first; no duties for 15 min
after.
