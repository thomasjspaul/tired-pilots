> **Secondary reference.** Every 703, 704 and 705 air operator must run a
> **fatigue-management training program** for its flight crew members. The
> requirement is
> [CARs 703.98(4)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.98.html)
> for air-taxi operations, **704.115(4)** for commuter operations and
> **705.124(4)** for airline operations — the wording is identical in all three.
> It sits inside the operator's Minister-approved ground and flight training
> program.

## What the program must cover

The regulation lists seven things the fatigue-management training program has to
contain (703.98(4)(a)–(g)):

- **Personal fatigue-management strategies** — sleep hygiene; lifestyle, exercise
  and diet; and the consumption of alcohol and drugs ((a)).
- **The impact of fatigue on aviation safety** ((b)).
- **Sleep requirements and the science relating to fatigue** ((c)).
- **The causes and consequences of fatigue** ((d)).
- **How to recognise fatigue** — in yourself and in others ((e)).
- **Sleep disorders** — their impact on aviation safety, and treatment options
  ((f)).
- **Human and organizational factors that may cause fatigue** — including sleep
  quality and duration, the impact of shift work and overtime, the circadian
  rhythm, and the effects of changing time zones ((g)).

## Who it applies to

The program is for the operation's **flight crew members**. Having it and
delivering it is the operator's obligation; the CARs fix the content but leave
the delivery method and the refresher interval to the operator's approved
training program. If your operator runs a
[Fatigue Risk Management System](/frms-exemptions), its training and promotion
elements build on this same base.

## Why it exists

The training requirement came in with the 2018 fatigue amendment
([SOR/2018-269](/changes)). The prescriptive [700.28](/rules/700-28) limits and
the [700.26](/rules/700-26) duty to declare when you are not fit for duty only
work if crews can recognise fatigue and manage it — this program is meant to give
everyone the same baseline for doing that.
