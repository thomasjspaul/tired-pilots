Every rule page and explainer shows a **"Last reviewed"** date — when it was last checked against the current CARs. This page collects the notable changes and the most recently reviewed pages.

## Notable changes

- **January 2026** — Site rebuilt from the long-running Google Site. New: plain-language pages for every operative section of Division III (700.19–700.72) with the verbatim regulation text; a [glossary](/glossary) of defined terms reproduced verbatim from CARs 101.01 and 700.01; an [FDP calculator](/calculator) covering 700.28, augmented crew (700.60) and split flight duty (700.50), with a citation on every step; an interactive [Increased Rest flowchart](/flowcharts/increased-rest); and secondary-reference pages for [Medevac](/medevac), [702 aerial work](/702-aerial-work) and [FRMS exemptions](/frms-exemptions).
