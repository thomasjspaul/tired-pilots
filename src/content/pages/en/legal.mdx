## Not legal advice

**tiredpilots.ca is an educational aid.** It is a plain-language guide to help
you find and understand the flight-crew fatigue rules — it is **not** legal
advice, and it is **not** a substitute for:

- the **Canadian Aviation Regulations** (SOR/96-433) as published on the
  [Justice Laws website](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/),
- **Advisory Circular 700-047** and other Transport Canada guidance, or
- your company **Operations Manual**, which may impose stricter limits.

It can contain errors, and the regulations can change. **Always verify against
the current CARs and your Operations Manual before you rely on anything here.**

## How it's sourced

- Each substantive statement cites the **CARs section** it comes from, and, where
  interpretation matters, the relevant part of **Advisory Circular 700-047**.
- Regulation wording is reproduced verbatim in the "Regulation text" expander on
  each rule page, and defined terms are reproduced verbatim in the
  [glossary](/glossary), from the Justice Laws website.
- Every page shows the date it was **last reviewed** against the regulations, and
  the date the regulation text was retrieved.
- Where the AC provides guidance that goes beyond the wording of the CARs, that is
  flagged as **guidance, not a rule**, and attributed to the AC.
