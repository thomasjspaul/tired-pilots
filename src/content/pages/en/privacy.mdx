**tiredpilots.ca** is a static reference site. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no
comment system, and no forms. It sets no advertising or tracking cookies, and it
does not sell or share personal information. This statement describes the little
that is collected and by whom.

It is written with Canadian privacy law in mind — principally the
[Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/p-8.6/)
and the guidance of the
[Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/). It is a
plain-language description, not legal advice.

## What this site collects

### Analytics

The site uses **Cloudflare Web Analytics**. It is **cookieless**, does not
fingerprint your device, and does not track you across other websites. It records
aggregate, non-identifying measurements — page views, referring sites, country,
browser and device type. Cloudflare processes this data on the site's behalf; see
[Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

### "Ask a question" search

The site has two search modes:

- **Keyword** search runs **entirely in your browser**. Nothing you type is sent
  anywhere.
- **Ask a question** (meaning-based) search sends **only the text of your query**
  to [Cohere](https://cohere.com), a Canadian artificial-intelligence company, to
  compute a numeric "meaning" vector used to rank pages. It does not send your
  identity, your IP address, or anything else. Per Cohere's API terms, text sent
  to its API is not used to train its models. The query text is briefly cached at
  Cloudflare's edge — keyed by the query itself, not by you — for up to 24 hours.

### "Suggest a correction" links

These open **your own email program** with a message pre-filled. Nothing is sent
unless you choose to send it. If you email a correction, that message and your
email address are received by the site operator and kept only as long as needed
to act on it.

### Your browser's local storage

One value is stored in your browser (`tp-disclaimer-ack-v1`) so the disclaimer
bar does not reappear after you dismiss it. It stays on your device and is never
transmitted.

### Server logs

The site is hosted on **Cloudflare Pages**. Cloudflare keeps standard,
short-lived request logs (such as IP address, timestamp and URL requested) for
security and reliability. The site operator does not use these to identify or
profile visitors.

## Children

The site is a general-audience professional reference. It is not directed at
children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Your rights

Under PIPEDA you have the right to ask an organisation what personal information
it holds about you and to request that it be corrected. Because this site holds
essentially no personal information about visitors, there is little to request —
but you may contact us at
[privacy@tiredpilots.ca](mailto:privacy@tiredpilots.ca) with any question.

If you are not satisfied with the response, you may file a complaint with the
**Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada** — [priv.gc.ca](https://www.priv.gc.ca/).
