> **Secondary reference.** This page summarises **Subpart 702** (aerial work). The [703/704/705 fatigue rules](/rules) — the tables, acclimatization, reserve, and so on — **do not apply** here (CARs 700.19(1); AC 700-047 § 4.1). 702 has its own, simpler regime in Division X, sections 702.91–702.98. The [authoritative text is on Justice Laws](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.91.html).

## Monitoring — 702.91

The air operator must have a system that monitors each flight crew member's **flight time, flight duty periods and rest periods**, with the details in the company operations manual. If anyone becomes aware that an assignment would exceed the 702.92 flight-time or 702.93 flight-duty-period limits, they must tell the operator as soon as possible.

## Flight time — 702.92

| Limit       | Window                                             |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| 1,200 hours | any 365 consecutive days                           |
| 300 hours   | any 90 consecutive days                            |
| 120 hours   | any 30 consecutive days — **100 hours** if on call |
| 60 hours    | any 7 consecutive days                             |
| 8 hours     | any 24 consecutive hours — single-pilot IFR only   |

These may be **exceeded only if** the extension is authorised in the air operator certificate and both parties comply with the **Commercial Air Service Standards** (702.92(2)). A crew member who reaches a limit can't stay on or be reassigned to flight duty until they've had the 702.93 rest or 702.96 time free from duty.

## Flight duty period — 702.93

- Maximum **14 consecutive hours in any 24 consecutive hours** (702.93(1)).
- **Split:** the FDP may be extended by **half the length of a rest period, up to 3 hours**, if the operator gives advance notice, a rest period of **at least 4 consecutive hours in suitable accommodation**, and doesn't interrupt it (702.93(3)). The following rest period is then extended by at least the amount of the extension (702.93(4)).
- A minimum rest period and any additional rest required by the Subpart follow every FDP (702.93(2)).

## Unforeseen operational circumstances — 702.94

The 702.92 flight time and the 702.93 maximum FDP may be exceeded if the flight is extended by an unforeseen operational circumstance after the FDP began, the **pilot-in-command** (after consulting the crew) considers it safe, and both parties comply with the Commercial Air Service Standards.

## Delayed reporting time — 702.95

If notified of a delay of **more than three hours** before leaving the rest facility, the FDP is treated as having begun **three hours after** the original reporting time.

## Time free from duty — 702.96

- **≥ 24 consecutive hours**: 13 times in any 90 days, and 3 times in any 30 days.
- **On call**: **≥ 36 consecutive hours** in any 7 days, or **≥ 3 consecutive days** in any 17 days. The operator must give notice of the start and duration.
- Alternatives to the 24-hour requirement are allowed only if authorised in the air operator certificate and both comply with the Commercial Air Service Standards.

## Positioning after an FDP — 702.97

If required to travel for positioning after an FDP, the operator provides an **additional rest period equal to at least half the travelling time that exceeds the maximum FDP**.

## Long-range flights — 702.98

- An FDP that ends **more than four one-hour time zones** from the point of departure (unless flown entirely within Northern Domestic Airspace) is limited to **three flights**, and is followed by a rest period **at least equal to the FDP**.
- If it includes a **transoceanic** flight, **only one** flight may follow it (excluding one unscheduled technical stop).
