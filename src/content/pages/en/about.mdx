**tiredpilots.ca** is an independent resource that explains Canada's flight-crew
fatigue rules — CARs Subpart 700, Division III — in plain language, for the
pilots who have to work to them.

It's built and run by a working pilot. It isn't affiliated with Transport
Canada, any air operator, any union, or any association, and it doesn't speak for
any of them.

## How to use it

- The **[rules by topic](/rules)** pages give you the plain-language version
  first, then the current in-force wording, with a link to the verbatim text and
  a citation to the exact CARs subsection.
- The **[glossary](/glossary)** reproduces the defined terms verbatim from CARs
  101.01 and 700.01.
- The **[FDP calculator](/calculator)** does the 700.28 table lookup for you and
  shows its working.
- The **[Q&A](/qa)** answers the questions that come up most.

## Accuracy and corrections

Every substantive statement cites the CARs section it comes from, and, where
guidance matters, the relevant part of **Advisory Circular 700-047**. The
regulation text was retrieved from the [Justice Laws website](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/)
and each page carries the date it was last checked.

This is an **educational aid, not legal advice**. It can contain errors and the
regulations can change. Always verify against the current CARs and your company
Operations Manual. Found a mistake? Use the **"Suggest a correction"** link at the
bottom of any page, or email
[corrections@tiredpilots.ca](mailto:corrections@tiredpilots.ca).
