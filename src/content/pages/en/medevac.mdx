> **Secondary reference.** Medical evacuation flights are covered by **Division IV
> of Subpart 700** (sections 700.100–700.135), **not** the
> [703/704/705 fatigue rules](/rules) — no 700.28 tables, no acclimatization
> mechanic. Positioning an aircraft **before or after** a medevac flight counts
> as a medevac flight (700.100(3)). The
> [authoritative text is on Justice Laws](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.100.html).

## Flight time — 700.103

| Limit       | Window                                                 |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 40 hours    | any 7 days — 704/705, aircraft other than a helicopter |
| 60 hours    | any 7 days — 703, or any helicopter                    |
| 120 hours   | any 30 days — **100 hours** if on call                 |
| 300 hours   | any 90 days                                            |
| 1,200 hours | any 365 days                                           |
| 8 hours     | any 24 hours — single-pilot only                       |

Flight time includes flying for other operations, and the whole flight's time on
an augmented-crew flight (700.103(2)).

## Flight duty period — 700.104

A flat maximum of **14 hours**. No start-time tables.

## Augmented crew — 700.131

| Maximum FDP | Rest facility               |
| ----------- | --------------------------- |
| 15 hours    | a flight deck observer seat |
| 17 hours    | class 2                     |
| 20 hours    | class 1                     |

One additional flight crew member, **three flights or fewer**, all rest-facility
time counted as FDP. If the FDP was extended this way, the following rest period
**equals the duration of the duty just completed** (700.131(4)).

## Rest — 700.116

**10 hours plus the travel time** to and from the place the rest is taken.
Advance notice required.

Related: **positioning rest** (700.117) and **split flight duty** (700.118) work
similarly to the 703/704/705 versions; see the
[Justice Laws text](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.117.html).

## Time free from duty — 700.119

One of: **≥ 36 consecutive hours** in any 7 days; **≥ 3 consecutive days** in any
17 days; or **≥ 4 consecutive days** in any 19 days. Advance notice required.

## Long-range flights — 700.132

An FDP that ends **more than four one-hour time zones** from the point of
departure (unless entirely within Northern Domestic Airspace) is limited to
**three flights** and followed by a rest period **at least equal to the FDP**. A
**transoceanic** flight may be followed by **only one** flight (excluding one
unscheduled technical stop).

## Controlled rest on the flight deck — 700.135

Same as [700.72](/rules/700-72): ≤ 45 minutes, in cruise, finished ≥ 30 minutes
before descent, one crew member at a time, ≥ 2 on the flight deck; hand over and
brief first, no duties for 15 minutes after.
