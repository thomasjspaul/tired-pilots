**700.64 (1)** In the event of an unforeseen operational circumstance that occurs
after the beginning of the flight duty period, an air operator may change a flight
crew member's flight duty period to include a split flight duty in accordance with
section 700.50 if the pilot-in-command agrees and the change is made before the
scheduled break on the ground.

**(2)** The pilot-in-command shall not agree to the change if they are of the
opinion, after consulting with all other crew members, that a split flight duty
period could lead to a level of fatigue that may adversely affect the safety of
the flight.
