**700.19 (1)** This Division does not apply

- **(a)** to an air operator who operates an aircraft under Subpart 2 of this
  Part or to a flight crew member who operates an aircraft under that Subpart; or
- **(b)** to an air operator who conducts a medical evacuation flight or to a
  flight crew member who operates an aircraft to conduct such a flight.

**(2)** For the purposes of this Division, references to a time of day are

- **(a)** if a flight crew member is acclimatized, references to the local time
  at their location; or
- **(b)** if a flight crew member is not acclimatized, references to the local
  time at the last location where the member was acclimatized.
