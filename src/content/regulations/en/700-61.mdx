Section 700.61 in full: _"An air operator shall not assign a flight duty period to a flight crew member, and a flight crew member shall not accept such an assignment, if the flight duty period occurs within the member's window of circadian low and includes a flight that follows a scheduled flight of more than seven hours."_

In other words: if any part of the FDP is in your [window of circadian low](/glossary#window-of-circadian-low) (02:00–05:59 acclimatized), you can't fly a **second** leg after a scheduled first leg longer than **seven hours**.

The AC adds that this applies to **un-augmented** flight crews, and that operating a >7-hour flight plus another WOCL-infringing flight with the same crew would need a **Fatigue Risk Management System** (AC 700-047 § 4.67). All **duty** — including positioning and dead-heading — counts toward the duty period.
