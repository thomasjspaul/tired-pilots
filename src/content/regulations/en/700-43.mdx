**Positioning** is defined in [CARs 101.01](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-101.01.html) as _"the transfer of a flight crew member from one location to another, at the request of an air operator, but does not include travel to or from suitable accommodation or the member's lodging."_ It is still work, and it keeps you awake. Section 700.43 sets the rest owed when a flight duty period **plus** the positioning that immediately follows it runs past the [700.28 maximum FDP](/rules/700-28). The mode of travel doesn't matter (AC 700-047 § 4.45).

Either way, the rest period can never be **shorter** than the normal [700.40(1)](/rules/700-40) rest (700.43(2); AC 700-047 § 4.46).

## Consent limits

The operator **cannot require** positioning that would push your FDP more than **3 hours** past the maximum unless (700.43(3)):

- you **agree** to it, and
- your FDP is not exceeded by **more than 7 hours**.

## Positioning with no flight to follow

If positioning is **not** immediately followed by a flight duty period, it is treated as a flight duty period for working out the [700.40](/rules/700-40) rest that follows (700.43(4)).
