**Standby** counts as [100% duty](/rules/700-29) — unlike reserve, which counts
at 33%.

## Facilities

The operator must provide a place that (700.71(1)):

- gives adequate protection from the elements,
- lets you sit,
- gives access to food and drink, and
- if possible, is **not** accessible to the public.

## Rest if you're not used

If standby ends **without** a flight duty assignment, you get the same rest as
after an FDP (700.71(2); AC 700-047 § 4.90):
