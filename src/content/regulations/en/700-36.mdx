Section 700.36 in full: _"An air operator shall assign a home base for each of its flight crew members."_

**[Home base](/glossary#home-base)** is defined in [CARs 101.01](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-101.01.html) as _"the location where a flight crew member normally commutes to in order to report for a flight duty period or positioning."_

## Why it matters

Home base is the anchor point for several other rules:

- the [rest period](/rules/700-40) options ("ends at home base" vs "away from home base");
- the [time-zone rest requirements](/rules/700-42), which treat outbound and return-to-base legs differently;
- the [acclimatization assumption](/rules/700-28#acclimatization) — an operator may assume you are acclimatized to your home-base time zone unless previous duty acclimatized you elsewhere (AC 700-047 § 4.23).
