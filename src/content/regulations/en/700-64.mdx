Section 700.63 covers the pilot-in-command **extending** an FDP for an [unforeseen operational circumstance](/rules/700-63). Section 700.64 is the narrower case where the operator instead wants to **turn the FDP into a [split flight duty](/rules/700-50)** part-way through.

## The conditions

The operator may make that change only if (700.64(1); AC 700-047 § 4.75):

- an **unforeseen operational circumstance** arose **after** the FDP began,
- the **pilot-in-command agrees**, and
- the change is made **before the scheduled ground break starts** — and the scheduled break must start _after_ the decision is made. Time spent waiting for the decision **doesn't count** toward the break.

The break itself is then credited under [700.50](/rules/700-50), but at the **unforeseen-circumstance rate — 50% of the break provided** (700.50(1)(c)).

## The PIC can say no

The PIC **shall not** agree if, after consulting all crew members on their fatigue, they think the split could cause fatigue that may affect the safety of the flight (700.64(2); AC 700-047 § 4.76).
