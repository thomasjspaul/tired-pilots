## What Division III doesn't cover

The fatigue rules in **Division III** (CARs 700.19–700.72 — the focus of this site) **don't apply** to the following (700.19(1)):

- **702 aerial-work** operations, which follow their own fatigue rules in [Division X of Subpart 702](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.91.html);
- **Medical evacuation flights**, covered by Division IV of Subpart 700 (the 700.100 series).

If your operator holds certificates for multiple subparts, the fatigue rules for the subpart under which a flight is operated apply to the entire flight (AC 700-047 § 4.1).

## "Time of day" means acclimatized local time

Every clock time reference in Division III—start times, the circadian low window, the 22:30–09:30 local night's rest window, and others—is based on **the location you're acclimatized to** (700.19(2)). If you're not acclimatized to your current location, it refers to the local time of the last place where you *were* acclimatized. This principle also applies to the [700.28 table lookup](/rules/700-28#acclimatization).
