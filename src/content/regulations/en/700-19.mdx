## What Division III doesn't cover

The fatigue rules in **Division III** (CARs 700.19–700.72 — the ones this site is
mostly about) **don't apply** to (700.19(1)):

- **702 aerial-work** operations — those have their own fatigue rules in
  [Division X of Subpart 702](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.91.html); or
- **medical evacuation flights** — covered by Division IV of Subpart 700 (the
  700.100 series).

If your operator holds certificates for more than one subpart, the fatigue rules
for the subpart a given flight is operated under apply for that whole flight (AC
700-047 § 4.1).

## "Time of day" means acclimatized local time

Every reference to a clock time in Division III — start times, the window of
circadian low, the 22:30–09:30 local night's rest window, and so on — is read at
**the location you're acclimatized to** (700.19(2)). If you're not acclimatized
where you are, it's the local time back at the last place you _were_ acclimatized.
This is the same principle behind the [700.28 table lookup](/rules/700-28#acclimatization).
