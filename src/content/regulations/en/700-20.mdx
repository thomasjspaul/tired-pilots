This is an **operator obligation**, but it's why you can ask for — and expect to
see — your own numbers.

## The monitoring system

The operator must have a system that monitors each flight crew member's **flight
times, flight duty periods, hours of work and rest periods**, and must set out how
that system works in its **company operations manual** (700.20(1)). Its purpose is
to make sure nobody is assigned to — or accepts — duty beyond the limits, and that
required rest and time free from duty are actually given (AC 700-047 § 4.3).

Where an operator flies under more than one subpart, the system has to capture
**all** of it across every subpart (AC 700-047 § 4.4).

## The records

For each flight crew member (700.20(2)):

- all flight times;
- start, end and duration of every **flight duty period**;
- start, end and duration of every **duty period**;
- start, end and duration of every **rest period**;
- all **time free from duty**.

Plus a record of **every** [pilot-in-command notification](/rules/700-63) of an
unforeseen operational circumstance under 700.63(4) — when, why and how the
provision was used (700.20(3); AC 700-047 § 4.5).

## Retention

All of it must be kept for **24 months** after the day the record is made
(700.20(4); AC 700-047 § 4.6).
