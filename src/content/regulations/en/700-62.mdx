Two absolute limits that sit above everything else:

- **No flight duty period over 18 hours** (700.62(1)). This is the ceiling even
  with an [augmented crew](/rules/700-60) — 18 hours is also the highest value in
  the 700.60 table.
- **No flight with a scheduled flight time over 16 hours** (700.62(2)).

Operating beyond either — an FDP over 18 hours, or a flight scheduled for more
than 16 hours of flight time — would require a **Fatigue Risk Management System**
(AC 700-047 §§ 4.68–4.69).

> **Interaction with split flight duty.** AC 700-047 § 4.49 says an FDP extended
> by [split flight duty](/rules/700-50) has "no limit" of its own, while 700.62(1)
> states a flat 18-hour ceiling. The regulations don't spell out how the two fit
> together — treat 18 hours as the ceiling and confirm against your Operations
> Manual.
