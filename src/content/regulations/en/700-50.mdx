Section 700.50 lets a flight duty period be scheduled **longer than the [700.28 maximum](/rules/700-28)** when the flight crew member is given a **break** in [suitable accommodation](/glossary#suitable-accommodation) partway through it.

> **On the term.** "Split flight duty" is **not a defined term** in the CARs (it is not in 101.01, 700.01 or 700.19) — it is only the heading placed over section 700.50. The regulation turns on a **break**, which AC 700-047 § 4.49 describes as _"a period of time on the ground, shorter than a rest period, when the flight crew is free of all duties but still considered to be within a flight duty period"_ — it counts as part of the FDP but not as hours worked.

## How much extra FDP the break buys

The break must be **at least 60 consecutive minutes**. Before the calculation, **45 minutes is subtracted** from the break (to cover getting to bed, falling asleep, and waking up — 700.50(2); AC 700-047 § 4.50). Then:

700.50(2) applies the 45-minute deduction "for the purposes of subsection (1)", i.e. to all three rows. AC 700-047 § 4.49 phrases the UOC case as "50% of the duration of the break provided"; this page follows the regulation text.

Worked through: a **4-hour** break overnight → (240 − 45) × 100% = **195 minutes** (3 h 15 m) of extra FDP. The same break during the day → (240 − 45) × 50% = **97.5 minutes**. A bare 60-minute break buys only 15 minutes (overnight) or 7.5 minutes (daytime). There is **no cap** on the total length of an FDP extended this way (AC 700-047 § 4.49).

## Limits and conditions

- The break must be in **suitable accommodation**; travel time to and from it is not part of the break.
- On **night duty**, split duty may extend the FDP for **at most three consecutive nights** (700.50(3)).
- No extra rest is required afterwards beyond the normal [700.40(1)](/rules/700-40) rest (AC 700-047 § 4.50(5)).
- **On reserve:** if assigned flight duty that includes split duty, the reserve duty period may be extended by **two hours**, and there may be **no more than two flights** after the break (700.50(5)).
