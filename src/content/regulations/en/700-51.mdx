This rule limits how many flight duty periods in a row may cut into the early-morning window **02:00–05:59** — the [window of circadian low](/glossary#window-of-circadian-low). Any FDP with _any part_ in that window counts (AC 700-047 § 4.54).

## The limit

- **Three** consecutive such FDPs is the default maximum. To assign a run of three, the operator must provide **one local night's rest at the end of the third** (700.51(1)).
- The run may go to **five** consecutive such FDPs only if the operator provides **both** (700.51(2); AC 700-047 § 4.55):
  1. a **3-hour rest period in suitable accommodation during each** flight duty period, and
  2. **56 consecutive hours free from duty** at the end of the last one.

After more than three and up to five such FDPs, no further duty may be assigned until the 56 hours free is complete.

## Interaction with split flight duty

A [night duty](/glossary#night-duty) by definition touches the window of circadian low, so a run of three night duties extended by [split flight duty](/rules/700-50) also hits this three-in-a-row limit (AC 700-047 § 4.54).
