When the operator tells you of a delay **before you leave your
[suitable accommodation](/glossary#suitable-accommodation)** to report, your
[maximum FDP](/rules/700-28) is worked out from **whichever of the original or the
delayed reporting time gives the shorter FDP** (700.52(1)). A delay can't be used
to _extend_ your maximum FDP (AC 700-047 § 4.56).

Worked (AC 700-047 § 4.57): initial report 06:00, delayed to 09:00 (3 h delay) →
FDP begins **09:00**. Initial report 06:00, delayed to 12:00 (6 h delay) → FDP
begins **10:00** (06:00 + 4 h).

## Delay of 10 hours or more

The delay counts as a **rest period** if (700.52(3)):

- you're told before you leave the suitable accommodation, and
- the operator doesn't disturb your rest before a time you've agreed.

If there's no agreed time, the operator may only contact you (700.52(4)):

- in the **30 minutes** before you were originally scheduled to leave the
  accommodation, or
- in the **60 minutes** before the original reporting time.
