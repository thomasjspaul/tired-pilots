A **[rest period](/glossary#rest-period)** is defined in
[CARs 101.01](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-101.01.html)
as _"the continuous period during which a flight crew member is off duty,
excluding the travel time to or from suitable accommodation provided by a private
operator or air operator."_

At home base the operator chooses one of the three options. The "11 hours plus
travel time" option means 11 hours at the place the rest is actually taken; the
operator must have a real means of measuring that travel time — it has to reflect
the time actually taken (700.40(3); AC 700-047 §§ 4.37, 4.39).

## Extra rest after over-long duty

If the operator keeps you on duty — anything other than flight duty — for a period
that runs **1 hour or more past** the maximum FDP from [section 700.28](/rules/700-28)
(not counting positioning), your rest period becomes the **longer** of:

- the maximum FDP **plus** the time worked beyond it, and
- the normal rest period from the table above.

(700.40(2); AC 700-047 § 4.38.)

> **Example (AC 700-047 § 4.38).** Your maximum FDP was 13 hours. After a 12-hour
> FDP the operator has you clean the aircraft for 2.5 hours — total duty 14.5
> hours, which is more than 1 hour past the 13-hour maximum. Your required rest
> period is now **14.5 hours**.

Operators should release flight crew from duty as soon as possible after the FDP
ends.

## Notice

The operator must give you **advance notice** of your rest period and how long it
is. Rest cannot be assigned after the fact (700.40(4); AC 700-047 § 4.40).
