**Hours of work** means all duty — flying, positioning, ground duties, training —
not just time in the air. "Work" and "duty" mean the same thing here (AC 700-047
§ 4.31).

## 60 or 70 hours in 7 days?

Which weekly cap applies depends on how much **time free from duty** your operator
gives you. During time free from duty you owe the operator nothing — not a phone
call, not a weather check, not availability (AC 700-047 § 4.32).

To **switch from the 60-hour option to the 70-hour option**, the operator must
first give you the full 120 consecutive hours free (including 5 consecutive local
nights' rest) before you go past the 60-hour limit (700.29(2); AC 700-047 § 4.33).

## Reserve and standby

- **Reserve** availability time counts at **33%** toward your hours of work.
- **Standby** time counts at **100%**.

(700.29(3); AC 700-047 § 4.34.) A reserve availability period scheduled to start
at 06:00 meets the definition of early duty, so it cannot be combined with the
70-hour option. Time spent on call under **aerial work (702)** or **medevac**
should be counted at 100% toward the 700.29 limit when you are also operating
under Division III.
