Some terms first, verbatim from [CARs 700.01](/glossary):

- A **flight crew member on reserve** is _"designated by an air operator to be available to report for flight duty on notice of more than one hour."_
- The **reserve availability period (RAP)** is _"the period in any period of 24 consecutive hours during which a flight crew member on reserve is available to report for flight duty."_
- The **reserve duty period (RDP)** _"begins at the time that a flight crew member on reserve is available to report for flight duty and ends at the time that the flight duty period ends."_ AC 700-047 § 4.84 summarises it as RAP + FDP = RDP.
- The **window of circadian low (WOCL)** is _"the period that begins at 02:00 and ends at 05:59 at the location where the flight crew member is acclimatized."_

## Notice and changing the start time

- **Notice of the RAP** (start, end, location): at least **12 hours** ahead, or **32 hours** ahead if any part of it falls in your WOCL (700.70(1)).
- The operator may shift the communicated start time by **no more than 2 hours earlier or 4 hours later** (700.70(2)(a)); and no more than **8 hours** either way within any 168 hours unless you get 2 consecutive days free from duty in that window (700.70(2)(b)).
- Moving the start past **02:00** blocks another RAP until you've had 2 consecutive days free (700.70(3)).
- Moving the start **into your WOCL** needs **24 hours' notice** (700.70(4)).

## Duration limits

- A **RAP** may not exceed **14 consecutive hours** (700.70(5)).
- At least **10 consecutive hours' rest** between RAPs (700.70(6)).

## Augmented crew and the quiet-WOCL extension

With an augmented crew and rest facilities, the RDP ceiling rises (700.70(8)): **20 h** (1 extra crew, class 1 or 2), **22 h** (2 extra crew, class 1 or 2, RAP starting 21:00–03:00 acclimatized), or **26 h** (2 extra crew, class 1, RAP starting before 21:00 or after 03:00).

If the RAP **starts between 02:00 and 05:59** acclimatized and the operator **doesn't contact you** during that window, the RDP may be extended by **2 hours or 50% of the RAP time falling in 02:00–05:59, whichever is shorter** (700.70(9); AC 700-047 § 4.86).

## Assigning an FDP longer than the RDP allows

The operator may assign an FDP that would exceed the RDP limit (or the [700.28 maximum](/rules/700-28), whichever is shorter) **only if all three** hold (700.70(10); AC 700-047 § 4.87):

1. at least **24 hours' notice** of the assignment before the FDP begins,
2. that notice is **not** given between **22:30 and 07:30**, and
3. **no duties** are assigned between the notice and the start of the FDP.
