A **disruptive schedule** flips you between **early duty** and **late or night
duty** (all defined in [CARs 700.01](/glossary): early duty starts 02:00–06:59,
late duty ends 00:00–01:59, night duty starts 13:00–01:59 and ends after 01:59 —
all at your acclimatized location). Because that forces your body clock to jump,
section 700.41 requires extra recovery on top of the normal
[rest period](/rules/700-40).

## What's required

When your schedule moves you **from late or night duty to early duty**, or **from
early duty to late or night duty**, the operator must give you a rest period that
**includes one local night's rest** (700.41(1); AC 700-047 § 4.41).

A **local night's rest** is at least nine hours between **22:30 and 09:30** at the
place you are acclimatized to.

"Duty" here means any activity that meets the definition of duty. Positioning
that is not immediately followed by a flight duty period is treated as a flight
duty period for working out rest (700.43(4)).

## When it doesn't apply

Section 700.41 does **not** apply when you are at a location more than **four
hours** off the local time where you were last acclimatized (700.41(2); AC
700-047 § 4.42). In that situation the [time-zone rest rules in 700.42](/rules/700-42)
take over.
