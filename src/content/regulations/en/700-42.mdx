These rules **replace** the normal [700.40 rest](/rules/700-40) when your flight
duty period crosses time zones. The more zones you cross, the longer the rest
(AC 700-047 §§ 4.43–4.44).

## Return to home base (FDP begins in another zone, ends at home base)

The timing runs from when you leave home base until you return. It depends on the
time-zone difference, how long you were away, and whether the return flight
touches your [window of circadian low](/rules/700-28#acclimatization) (02:00–05:59
acclimatized).
