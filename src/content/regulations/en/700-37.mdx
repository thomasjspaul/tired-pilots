Section 700.37 in full: _"An air operator shall provide a flight crew member
with not less than 15 minutes every 6 hours within a flight duty period to eat
and drink."_

The nutrition break is time **within** the flight duty period — the FDP clock
keeps running through it. It is not a [rest period](/rules/700-40) or a
[split-duty break](/rules/700-50).
