With an **augmented flight crew** and an on-board **rest facility**, the maximum
flight duty period comes from this table instead of [700.28](/rules/700-28)
(700.60(1)).

<AugmentedCrewTable />

**Rest facility classes** (AC 700-047 § 4.60):

- **Class 1** — a bunk or other horizontal surface, separate from the flight deck
  and passenger cabin, with temperature and light control and minimal noise.
- **Class 2** — a seat allowing a horizontal sleeping position, separated from
  passengers by a curtain or similar that reduces light and sound, with portable
  oxygen equipment. (A bunk that isn't a seat and doesn't meet class 1 counts as
  class 2.)
- **Class 3** — a seat that reclines at least 40° from vertical, with leg and
  foot support.

## Conditions

The extended FDP applies only to a flight duty period with **three or fewer
flights**, and only if in-flight rest is provided (700.60(2); AC 700-047 § 4.61):

- **One flight:** all flight crew members get in-flight rest in the rest facility.
- **Two or three flights:** the crew member who will land the aircraft on the
  final landing gets **two consecutive hours** of in-flight rest; every other
  flight crew member gets **90 consecutive minutes**.

Other conditions:

- All time spent in the rest facility counts as flight duty period (700.60(3)).
- The FDP begins and ends at the same location for everyone. Exception: if the
  first flight is scheduled under **105 minutes**, extra crew may join after it —
  but all end at the same location, and at least one additional crew member is on
  the flight deck for every take-off and landing after the first (700.60(4),(5)).
- In-flight rest happens between reaching **10,000 ft (3,048 m)** above aerodrome
  elevation and **15 minutes before** the scheduled start of descent (700.60(6)).

## Rest afterwards

If the FDP was extended under this section, the rest period is the **longest** of
(700.60(7); AC 700-047 § 4.66):

- the duration of the duty period just completed,
- **16 hours** if the FDP ends at home base, or
- **14 hours** in suitable accommodation.

So an 18-hour augmented FDP requires an 18-hour rest period; a 14-hour one ending
at home base requires 16 hours.
