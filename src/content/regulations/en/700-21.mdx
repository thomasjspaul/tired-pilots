Another **operator obligation** — but it's the one that's supposed to stop a
schedule from quietly depending on you being extended every trip.

## Enough notice to plan rest

The operator must give you your schedule **sufficiently in advance to plan for
adequate rest** (700.21(1)). There's no fixed number, but the AC says the notice
should be **longer** for disruptive duties (early, late, night) or duties that hit
the [window of circadian low](/glossary#window-of-circadian-low), and points to
the [reserve notice periods in 700.70](/rules/700-70) (12 h / 32 h) as a
reasonable benchmark (AC 700-047 § 4.7).

## The 10% check

Each **month**, the operator must check whether a given flight's
[maximum FDP](/rules/700-28) is being exceeded **more than 10% of the time over
any 90 consecutive days** (700.21(2)).

If that threshold is crossed **because of unforeseen operational circumstances**,
the operator must change the **schedule or the crew pairing** for that flight
**within 28 days** of the finding (700.21(3)). If schedules are set seasonally,
the fix may wait until the same season next year (700.21(4)).
