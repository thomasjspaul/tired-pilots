A **disruptive schedule** — the heading of [section 700.41](/rules/700-41), not a defined term — is a duty pattern that **flips between early duty and late or night duty**. The three duty types are defined in [CARs 700.01](/glossary), all measured at your acclimatized location:

- **early duty** — hours of work that **begin 02:00–06:59**;
- **late duty** — hours of work that **end 00:00–01:59**;
- **night duty** — hours of work that **begin 13:00–01:59 and end after 01:59**.

## What's required

Going **from late/night duty to the following early duty**, or **from early duty to the following late/night duty**, the operator must provide — **on top of** the [700.40](/rules/700-40) rest period — **one local night's rest** (CARs 700.41(1)).

A **[local night's rest](/glossary#local-nights-rest)** is a rest period of at least **nine hours between 22:30 and 09:30** at your acclimatized location (CARs 700.01).

## The exception

Section 700.41 **doesn't apply** when you're at a location more than **four hours** off the local time where you were last acclimatized. There, the [time-zone rest rules in 700.42](/rules/700-42) take over (CARs 700.41(2); AC 700-047 § 4.42).
