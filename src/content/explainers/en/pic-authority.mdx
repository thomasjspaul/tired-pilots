When an [unforeseen operational circumstance](/rules/700-63) (UOC) arises within
60 minutes of the start of, or during, a flight duty period, [CARs 700.63(1)](/rules/700-63)
says the **pilot-in-command** — _"if the pilot-in-command is of the opinion"_ that
it could cause unsafe fatigue — may, **after consulting all crew members** on
their fatigue:

- **reduce** a flight crew member's FDP,
- **extend** it beyond the [700.28](/rules/700-28) or [700.60](/rules/700-60)
  maximum (1 h single-pilot, 2 h two-pilot, 3 h augmented with one flight, 2 h
  augmented with two or three flights), or
- **extend** the rest period.

## The operator's role

The authority _"rests solely with the Pilot-In-Command"_ (AC 700-047 § 4.71). The
operator:

- **may** provide reasonable guidance and set boundaries that are **more
  restrictive** than the regulation,
- **may not** make the decision for the PIC, and
- has no defined limit in the regulation on how far the PIC may _reduce_ an FDP
  or _extend_ rest — that's left to the PIC's judgement (AC 700-047 § 4.71).

## If the circumstance was foreseeable

If the event was known **more than 60 minutes** before reporting, it is **not**
an unforeseen operational circumstance and the maximum FDP **cannot** be extended
on that basis (AC 700-047 § 4.70).

## After the fact

At the end of the FDP the PIC must **notify the operator** of any change made
under 700.63, and the operator must extend the following rest period by **at
least** the amount of the extension (CARs 700.63(3), (4)).
