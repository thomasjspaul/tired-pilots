**"Continuous duty"** is not a term in the CARs. Pilots use it for a common pattern: report in the afternoon or evening, fly to an outstation, **wait there for hours**, then fly back early the next morning.

## The wait is duty, not rest

That wait sits **inside your [flight duty period](/glossary#flight-duty-period)**. It is not a [rest period](/glossary#rest-period) — a rest period is a continuous period _off duty_ — and it is not time free from duty. So:

- Your **maximum FDP still comes from [700.28](/rules/700-28)**, keyed to your **evening report time**. Evening starts sit in the more restrictive rows, so the limit is short — often 12 hours or less.
- The clock keeps running through the wait.

## The only way to break it up

A qualifying **[split flight duty](/rules/700-50)** break — at least 60 minutes in **[suitable accommodation](/glossary#suitable-accommodation)** — is the one mechanism that both stops counting against you the same way _and_ lets the FDP be extended. A wait in a crew room or a terminal is neither.

If the trip flips you between early and late/night duty, the [disruptive-schedule rule (700.41)](/rules/700-41) also applies.
